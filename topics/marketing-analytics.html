TOPIC · MARKETING ANALYTICS

Marketing analytics: where should the next rupee or dollar go?

Which marketing spend is actually driving growth? Our marketing analytics writing explains how to measure what marketing contributes, and shows the same methods at work far beyond marketing.

Attribution, marketing mix modelling and controlled experiments each answer a different question, and most leadership teams are sold one of them as if it answered all three. Our guide to marketing ROI sets out which method to trust for which decision, and how to turn the answer into budgets that a finance team will accept.

We also apply marketing analytics to public questions. Pharmaceutical companies in India know, town by town, where their medicines sell and which channels persuade doctors and patients. Read the other way, that commercial data can show where people go untreated, which vaccine messages work, and where an outbreak may be starting. Our research essays make that case with sources shown and limits stated.

The questions we explore

  • Which channels are driving incremental growth, and which are taking credit for it?
  • When should we use marketing mix modelling, attribution or experiments?
  • How should budget move between brands, channels and regions next year?
  • What could commercial data tell public health that its own systems cannot?
